At the table

  1. Set up the tablePick avatars, enter optional names, choose All you can eat or Kaiten (Pro) and turn on In the middle of the table when friends sit across from you.
  2. Keep count togetherWhen In the middle of the table is on, the far side's lanes turn 180° so everyone reads upright and taps from their own seat. Counts draw as groups of five tally strokes with a vermilion seal for the leader.
  3. Watch the restaurant clockEnable Time limit before starting or from the More menu to track remaining minutes, see the last-order mark and receive local notifications before last order and at the end.
  4. Finish on the slipTap Finish to see standings, titles (Sushi champion, First bite, Last bite, Biggest bill) and the How the night went chart. Share the slip as an image or share the bill split as text.

Questions & Answers

What is free and what is Sushi Night Pro?

Free: two diners at an all-you-can-eat dinner, In the middle of the table facing both sides, tally marks and seals, Take one back and Undo, Time limit with local last-order alerts, and the closing slip with How the night went and Share slip. Sushi Night Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase (no subscription) unlocking up to 12 diners, Kaiten plate colors and prices, Who pays what (with drinks, shared extras, set price per person and service), Your places and Past dinners with all-time records.

Why did no time-limit notification arrive?

Local alerts only fire when a Time limit is turned on and notification permission is allowed. If you refused permission, the sitting clock still counts on screen. You can turn notifications on in iOS Settings > Notifications > Sushi Night.

Where do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the Pro sheet or Settings (the gear icon on the setup screen) and tap Restore Purchase while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Counting, saved dinners, saved places and local alerts work offline on your device. Internet access is only used by Apple StoreKit when fetching prices, buying or restoring Sushi Night Pro.

Are the bill amounts official?

No. Amounts on the slip and Who pays what are an estimate for the table; the restaurant's bill is always what counts.
