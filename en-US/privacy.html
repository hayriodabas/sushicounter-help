On-device storage

Your active table, setup preferences (sound and haptics toggles), saved restaurants and past dinner slips remain exclusively on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is uploaded or shared with us or third parties.

Permissions

Notifications (optional) are scheduled locally on your device solely for the restaurant time-limit and last-order reminders. If you choose Save Image inside the iOS share sheet for a slip image, iOS uses add-only photo library write permission; Sushi Night never reads your photo library or uses the camera.

In-app purchase

Sushi Night Pro (app.klarwerk.sushicounter.unlock) is a single non-consumable purchase handled directly by Apple through StoreKit 2. We never see your payment details or Apple Account.
